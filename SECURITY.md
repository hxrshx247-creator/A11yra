# Security policy

Report a suspected vulnerability privately by [emailing the repository maintainer](mailto:hxrshx247@gmail.com) at the address published in the commit history. Include the affected version or URL, the impact, and steps using synthetic data. Keep real documents, credentials, and reader links out of the report.

A11yra stores original and working DOCX files and audit data in private storage. The database holds accounts, memberships, document metadata, findings and evidence snippets, and records of repairs, exports, and activity. A published reader snapshot is accessible to anyone holding its valid link. Teachers can delete a document or revoke a reader link.

Working files expire after 24 hours by default. The default audit-history cap is 30 days, with 7 days for free workspaces and 30 days for paid plans. Workspace activity also defaults to 30 days. See the [data inventory](docs/DATA-INVENTORY.md) and [published reader security notes](docs/SECURITY-NOTES.md).
